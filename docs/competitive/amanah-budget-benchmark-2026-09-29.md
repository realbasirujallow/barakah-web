# Amanah Budget Benchmark — 2026-09-29

## Evidence reviewed

- Amanah Budget public home page, user guide, press kit, App Store listing, and update log, reviewed 2026-09-29.
- Barakah production surfaces and source code, reviewed 2026-09-29.

This is an internal product benchmark, not a public comparison page. Competitor claims must be rechecked before public use.

## Amanah's visible strengths

- Clear halal-first positioning and polished feature education.
- iOS and Android distribution, bank sync, budgets, goals, family sharing, zakat, riba awareness, and halal-investing material.
- Strong trust language: no ads, no data selling, methodology and update pages.
- Clear free / Pro / Family pricing and feature limits.

## Barakah's defensible differentiation

| Differentiator | What Barakah can substantiate | Proof surface | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Connected inheritance planning | Faraid calculator, Wasiyyah planning, Waqf records, household data, assets and debts in one workspace | `/faraid-calculator`, `/dashboard/faraid`, `/methodology` | Planning aid only; no legal advice or personal fatwa |
| Explainable alerts and auditability | Links from alerts to supporting records and an Audit Ledger for financial change history | `/dashboard/ledger`, dashboard alerts and recurring/budget views | Do not claim an alert is infallible or a compliance verdict |
| Inspectable methodology | Public methodology, changelog, scholar-review boundaries and transparent fiqh caveats | `/methodology`, `/methodology/changelog`, `/trust` | Never imply scholar endorsement before written approval |

## Positioning decision

Do not compete by asserting that Barakah is the only halal budgeting app. Compete on a narrower, provable promise:

> Islamic household finance tools that show the calculation, the record behind the alert, and the planning path for your family.
